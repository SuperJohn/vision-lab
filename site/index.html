Vision Lab

A private, fully on-device multimodal lab: camera, mic and screen feed real-time detection and a local VLM, all on one Mac Studio. Nothing leaves the machine.

View the repo on GitHub

What it does

Fast loop

YOLO11 detect/segment/pose + ByteTrack at ~30 fps (≈5 ms/frame) with zones and rules.

Slow loop

gemma4 VLM captions keyframes and screen captures; SigLIP embeddings power text search.

Voice

Local ASR, LLM and TTS with sub-second replies.

Privacy mode

One server-confirmed switch pauses camera, mic and screen capture across every process.

Detections in action

Real keyframes from the lab camera with detector boxes (conf ≥ 0.5). Frames were chosen and screened for privacy by a local model, then checked by hand.

Architecture

Everything runs on a single Mac Studio (M3 Ultra).

Data quality

Couch false-positive rate

A 0.5 confidence floor cuts flagged couch detections from 37.9% to 1.4% (2,012 → 1,300 detections).

Person tracking

Duplicate boxes in a frame are almost absent, but 13% of person tracks have a likely duplicate ID. That's the next fix (ByteTrack buffer / re-ID).

Local model bake-off

This site is a multimodal stress test

The site itself was assembled locally: a VLM ranked and privacy-screened candidate frames, then annotation, video encoding and charts all ran on the same machine.
